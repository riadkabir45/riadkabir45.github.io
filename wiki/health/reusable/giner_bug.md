# Ginger Bug & Soda Guide

*A small culture of probiotics that is easy to maintain and boosts health*

## Phase 1: The "Birth" of the Bug (The Starter)

**Container:** 100ml Glass Coffee Jar  
**Lid Status:** LOOSE (Resting on top, not twisted)  
**Goal:** Grow a colony of healthy, eye-benefitting probiotics.

### Day 1: The Setup

1. **Sanitize:** Wash the jar and plastic lid thoroughly with soap.
2. **The Mix:** * **Ginger:** 1 tbsp finely diced (Keep the skin ON).
  - **Sugar:** 1 tbsp (Preferably Palm Sugar or other Unprocessed Sugar).
  - **Water:** Fill 3/4 with filtered or boiled-then-cooled water.
3. **The Placement:** Keep in a dark corner away from sunlight. Place in a shallow dish of water (ant moat).

### Day 2 to Day 5: The Daily Feed

- **Action:** Every 24 hours, add **1 tsp diced ginger** and **1 tsp sugar**.
- **Stir:** Stir vigorously with a clean spoon for 30 seconds to add oxygen.
- **Signs of Success:** Look for tiny bubbles, a "fizzing" sound when stirred, and a smell like fresh bread/yeast.

---

## Phase 2: Making the Soda (The Fizz)

**Container:** Reused Plastic Cola Bottle (PET)  
**Lid Status:** AIRTIGHT (Twisted as tight as possible)

1. **Prep Liquid:** Fill your bottle with boxed juice (Pran/Shezan) or homemade ginger-sugar water. Leave 2 inches of air space at the top.
2. **Inoculate:** Strain and add **2-3 tbsp** of your active Ginger Bug liquid into the bottle.
3. **The Squeeze Test:** * Check every 6–12 hours.
  - When the plastic bottle feels **rock hard** like a car tire, it is ready.
4. **The Release:** Open slowly over a sink. In Dhaka's heat, this happens fast (usually 12–24 hours).

---

## Phase 3: Maintenance & "Back-Slopping"

**Goal:** Keeping the culture alive with zero waste.

- **Continuous Loop:** When your bug jar gets full of ginger, strain out the liquid into a clean container. Throw the old ginger into your **Curry Cooker** or tea.
- **The Restart:** Pour half the old liquid back into the 100ml jar. Add fresh water, 1 tsp ginger, and 1 tsp sugar.
- **Break Mode:** If you aren't making soda, just add 1 tsp sugar every 2 days. Add fresh ginger once a week to prevent mold.

---

## Tips for Success

- **Eye Health Hack:** Use **Palm Sugar**. The minerals help with retinal pressure and digital eye strain.
- **Halal Safety:** Drink your soda within 48 hours of it becoming fizzy. If it smells like strong wine/alcohol, throw it out and start over.
- **Water Quality:** Never use raw Dhaka tap water (chlorine kills the bug). Always use filtered or boiled water.
- **Ant Protection:** Always use the "Ant Moat" (a saucer of water under the jar) because the sugar will attract them quickly.

---

## Things to AVOID

1. **NEVER** tighten the lid on the glass coffee jar (Explosion risk).
2. **NEVER** use metal containers (Reaction with acid).
3. **NEVER** use juice with "Sodium Benzoate" (Kills the probiotics).
4. **NEVER** scrub the plastic soda bottles with wire—it creates scratches where bad bacteria hide. Replace the bottle after 3–4 uses.
